# Legal and ethical analysis
Status: completed scenario-level analysis; not legal advice.

Under the stipulated employee work-for-hire facts, the smaller firm is the copyright owner absent a contrary signed agreement. A separate work-for-hire agreement is not required for qualifying employee work. The written agreement requirement for certain commissioned works is distinct. The larger firm's employment agreement cannot convey rights in the former employer's existing code. Contribution, possession, modification, internal use and lack of resale do not themselves authorize reproduction or adaptation. Independent development using general knowledge is distinct from copying protected expression; legal review should determine applicable exceptions and facts.

## Governance implications
Define ownership and scope during onboarding, verify third-party software provenance and licensing before installation, and use written licensing terms where appropriate. Do not confuse preventive deficiencies with forfeiture of legal rights. Management should not override legal obligations through risk acceptance.
