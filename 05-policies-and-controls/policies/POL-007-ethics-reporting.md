# Sage Assurance | Ethics, Whistleblower and Non-Retaliation Policy

Document ID: POL-007 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Independent Oversight / HR | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory ethics, whistleblower and non-retaliation requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Provide confidential reporting outside implicated management.
- acknowledge reports promptly.
- preserve evidence.
- investigate independently.
- prohibit retaliation and monitor adverse employment actions.
- communicate outcomes where lawful.

## Accountability
Independent Oversight / HR maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Reporting register, investigation records, retaliation reviews. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-003, R-008. Proposed framework alignment: NIST CSF GV.RR, GV.PO. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
