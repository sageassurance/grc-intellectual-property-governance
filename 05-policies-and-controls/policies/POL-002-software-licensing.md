# Sage Assurance | Software Acquisition and Licensing Policy

Document ID: POL-002 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: IT Asset Management | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory software acquisition and licensing requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Require a request, provenance verification, legal license review and security approval before installation.
- deploy through managed channels.
- reconcile inventory monthly.
- quarantine unapproved software with evidence preservation.

## Accountability
IT Asset Management maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Approval tickets, licenses, inventory reconciliation, installation logs. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-001, R-002, R-006, R-010. Proposed framework alignment: NIST CM-8, CM-10, CM-11; ISO A.8.19. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
