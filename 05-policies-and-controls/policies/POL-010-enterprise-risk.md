# Cierra Jennings | Enterprise Risk Management Policy

Document ID: POL-010 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Enterprise Risk | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory enterprise risk management requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Record risk scenarios, owners and evidence.
- score likelihood and impact 1–5.
- prioritize scores 20–25 as critical.
- select treatment and deadlines.
- reassess residual risk using validated controls.
- escalate critical risks and review quarterly.

## Accountability
Enterprise Risk maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Risk register, treatment plans, review minutes, acceptance decisions. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-001 through R-010. Proposed framework alignment: NIST CSF GV.RM, ID.RA; ISO 31000. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
