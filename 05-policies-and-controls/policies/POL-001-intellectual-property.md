# Sage Assurance | Intellectual Property Protection Policy

Document ID: POL-001 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Legal | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory intellectual property protection requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Document ownership and permitted use at creation.
- verify rights before copying or adapting external code.
- maintain provenance and license records.
- restrict repository access.
- report suspected IP loss immediately.

## Accountability
Legal maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Ownership register, repository access reviews, license records. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-001, R-004, R-006. Proposed framework alignment: ISO A.5.32, A.8.4; NIST AC-6. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
