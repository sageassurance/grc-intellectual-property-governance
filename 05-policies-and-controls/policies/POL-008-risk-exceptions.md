# Sage Assurance | Risk Acceptance and Exception Management Policy

Document ID: POL-008 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Enterprise Risk | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory risk acceptance and exception management requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Require documented scope, rationale, risk rating, compensating controls, owner and expiration.
- independent approval for elevated risks.
- prohibit exceptions to legal obligations.
- review monthly and revoke expired exceptions.

## Accountability
Enterprise Risk maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Exception register, approval records, expiry reviews. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-003, R-005, R-008. Proposed framework alignment: NIST CSF GV.RM, GV.OV. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
