# Cierra Jennings | Employee Onboarding and Offboarding Policy

Document ID: POL-005 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: HR | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory employee onboarding and offboarding requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Explain IP obligations before access.
- obtain acknowledgment.
- provision role-based access.
- review access on transfers.
- revoke access at separation effective time.
- recover assets and document exceptions.
- preserve records under legal hold.

## Accountability
HR maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Onboarding checklist, access tickets, asset return and revocation logs. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-004, R-008. Proposed framework alignment: NIST PS-4, PS-6, AC-2; ISO A.5.11. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
