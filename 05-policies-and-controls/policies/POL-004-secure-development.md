# Sage Assurance | Secure Software Development Policy

Document ID: POL-004 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Engineering | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory secure software development requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Record source provenance and dependencies.
- require peer review and approved repositories.
- restrict write access.
- scan dependencies and licenses before release.
- require documented approval of external code.

## Accountability
Engineering maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Pull requests, dependency inventory, scan results, release approvals. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-004, R-006. Proposed framework alignment: NIST SA-10, SA-11, AC-6; ISO A.8.4. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
