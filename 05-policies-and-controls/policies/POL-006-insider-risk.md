# Sage Assurance | Insider Risk Management Policy

Document ID: POL-006 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Security | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory insider risk management requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Assess role-based exposure.
- monitor authorized security telemetry proportionately.
- investigate alerts with HR and legal.
- prohibit discriminatory profiling.
- restrict investigation access.
- document decisions and protect reporters.

## Accountability
Security maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Risk assessments, alert triage records, investigation access logs. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-003, R-004, R-008. Proposed framework alignment: NIST PM-12, AU-6, AC-6. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
