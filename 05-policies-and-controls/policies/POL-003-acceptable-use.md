# Cierra Jennings | Acceptable Use Policy

Document ID: POL-003 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Information Security | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory acceptable use requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Use approved systems and software only.
- prohibit importing former employer code without authorization.
- prohibit bypassing installation restrictions.
- report suspected violations.
- acknowledge rules at onboarding and annually.

## Accountability
Information Security maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Acknowledgments, incident tickets, endpoint logs. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-002, R-004, R-006. Proposed framework alignment: NIST PL-4, CM-11. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
