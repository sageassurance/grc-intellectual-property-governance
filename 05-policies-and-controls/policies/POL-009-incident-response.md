# Cierra Jennings | Information Security Incident Response Policy

Document ID: POL-009 | Version: 1.0 | Status: Proposed simulation draft | Owner: Security Incident Response | Approval: Pending independent executive approval | Review: Annual and after material change

## Purpose and scope
Establish mandatory information security incident response requirements for the simulated enterprise, including employees, contractors, managed systems and relevant third parties. No actual adoption is claimed.

## Mandatory requirements
- Triage suspected unauthorized software.
- contain with legal and IT.
- preserve evidence and chain of custody.
- determine scope.
- coordinate required notifications.
- recover through approved replacement or license.
- conduct lessons learned.

## Accountability
Security Incident Response maintains this policy and its evidence. Managers enforce requirements; personnel report violations; GRC monitors compliance; legal advises on obligations; independent oversight resolves conflicts and approves material changes.

## Exceptions and enforcement
Document exceptions before use with scope, justification, compensating controls, owner, expiry and independent approval under POL-008. No exception authorizes unlawful activity. Investigate violations fairly with HR and legal; apply proportionate corrective action and protect reporters.

## Assurance
Required evidence: Incident timeline, evidence log, notification decisions, recovery tests. GRC reviews evidence at least annually and after material incidents; missing evidence is recorded as unverified, not a pass. Retain records under the approved retention schedule and legal holds.

## Traceability
Risks: R-001, R-002, R-004, R-009. Proposed framework alignment: NIST IR-4, IR-6, IR-8. Mapping is illustrative and requires validation against the applicable framework text.
