# Preventive governance and control design
Status: proposed design, not implemented or tested.

| Control | Proposed requirement | Owner | Test / evidence | Mapping |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| IP onboarding | Define job scope, ownership and prior-employer IP restrictions; obtain acknowledgment | HR / Legal | Sample signed acknowledgments | ISO A.5.32 |
| Software acquisition | Require provenance, license and security approval before deployment | IT / Legal | Sample approved installation tickets and licenses | CM-10, CM-11; ISO A.8.19 |
| Source-code protection | Least privilege, access reviews, logging and approved transfer channels | Security | Review access grants and transfer logs | AC-6; ISO A.8.4 |
| Offboarding | Revoke access, recover assets and document retention obligations | HR / IT | Sample exit records and revocation timestamps | PS-4; ISO A.5.11 |
| Inventory | Reconcile installed software with approved assets | IT asset management | Compare inventory with approval records | CM-8 |
| Incident escalation | Confidential reporting, independent review, evidence preservation and non-retaliation | GRC / HR / Legal | Tabletop exercise and escalation records | IR-4 |
| Management exceptions | Require independent documented approval; prohibit exceptions to legal obligations | Executive oversight | Sample exception decisions | Governance process |

Preventive controls should operate throughout employment, not solely at departure. Training, technical enforcement and leadership accountability complement written agreements. Full policy documents and operating-effectiveness testing remain planned.
