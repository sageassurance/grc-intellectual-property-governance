# Harborline Public Services | Cybersecurity assurance

**Portfolio author** Cierra Jennings

Harborline is a fictional provider of a municipal casework portal. This case study examines how the company could protect resident records, reduce third-party exposure, and verify that its security controls work.

| Deliverable | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| [Security policy suite](harborline-security-policy-suite.md) | Acceptable use, incident response, and access control policies with verification criteria |
| [Cybersecurity assurance workbook](harborline-cybersecurity-assurance-workbook.xlsx) | A gap assessment covering 93 ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls, all seven NIST RMF stages, a ten-risk register, four weighted vendor assessments and awareness metrics |
| [Awareness program and results report](harborline-awareness-program-and-results.md) | Role-based training, an offline mock phishing exercise, scripted results and a corrective-action retest |

The assessments and outcomes are fictional. The Annex A mapping is a practice assessment. Proposed residual risk ratings await evidence in the scenario. The awareness results are scripted and no live phishing campaign took place.
