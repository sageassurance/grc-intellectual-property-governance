# GRC Project Portfolio - Cierra Jennings

Hands-on defensive cybersecurity GRC practice in risk assessment, control assessment, corrective action tracking and audit preparation for Meridian, a fictional 120-person logistics company.

## Projects

### 1. Risk Register

- **Scope** Meridian’s workforce identities, VPN, customer shipping file server, vulnerability management and ShipDesk delivery-data processing.
- **Method** Identified risks from fictional access review, scan, policy/process and vendor observations. Scored likelihood × impact on 1–5 inputs. Score bands are 1–7 Low, 8–14 Medium and 15–25 High. Scores are exercise judgments. Verified residual ratings await evidence.
- **Result** Six risks logged, five High. Each has an owner, response strategy and target date.
- **File** [risk-register.csv](risk-register.csv)

### 2. Control Assessment

- **Framework** [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final). The control bank uses exercise-specific criteria mapped to selected control IDs.
- **Method** Examined scenario evidence. Identified interviews as follow-up and specified independent tests in the corrective actions. Every control has an evidence reference.
- **Result** Seven controls assessed and six distinct findings recorded.
- **Files** [control-assessment.csv](control-assessment.csv) · [control-bank.csv](control-bank.csv) · [evidence-index.md](evidence-index.md)

### 3. Corrective Action Plan Tracker

- **Method** Linked each finding to a documented fix, owner, interim measure, original due date and verification method.
- **Result** Six actions tracked, four in progress and two open. Two are overdue as of the simulated status date. Zero are verified closed.
- **File** [corrective-action-plan.csv](corrective-action-plan.csv)

### 4. Vendor Documentation and Audit Package

- **ShipDesk** Documented customer data access, renewal and contract gaps, missing assurance, and standing support access. [Vendor assessment](vendor-assessment.csv)
- **Audit in 30 days** Reconciled risks, controls, evidence and corrective actions. Documented the overdue explanations without changing the baseline due dates. [Audit status summary](audit-status-summary.md)
- **Exports** The five linked CSVs are ready to import into spreadsheet or GRC tools.

## Skills demonstrated

- Risk identification, scoring, and treatment planning
- Control assessment against NIST SP 800-53
- Finding writing with criteria, observation, and impact
- Third-party risk documentation
- Remediation tracking and independent verification planning
- Audit evidence traceability and status reporting

## Notes

All work uses Meridian and the fictional scenario records. Status and dates are simulated. No real employer data, system tests or audit sign-off are included.
