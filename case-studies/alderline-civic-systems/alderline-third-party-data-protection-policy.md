# Third-Party Data Protection Policy

**Company** Alderline Civic Systems, a fictional govtech vendor  
**Policy** POL-001 · Draft v0.1 · Prepared by Cierra Jennings  
**Owner** Security & Risk Lead  
**Approver** COO after Security, Procurement and Legal review  
**Effective date** Pending approval and publication  
**Review cycle** Annually and after a material change, vendor incident, or change in the data a vendor handles

## Purpose and scope

Alderline runs a cloud-based permit and service request portal for municipalities. This policy applies to any supplier or subcontractor that stores, transmits, processes, or can access city, applicant, or employee data. It covers that access from purchase or trial through contract termination.

## Before a vendor gets access

1. The business owner records what data the vendor will receive, why it needs it, where it will be stored, who can access it, and how long it will be kept. Use the smallest data set that meets the business need.
2. Security & Risk assigns a tier. **Critical** vendors host restricted production data or provide a service essential to portal recovery. **High** vendors process resident information or have privileged access. **Moderate** vendors receive limited operational data without privileged access.
3. Security & Risk reviews the vendor’s evidence for safeguards relevant to the service, including restricted access, MFA for privileged accounts, encryption, logging, incident response, and recovery where applicable. Record the evidence date, scope, limitations, and decision.
4. Legal and Procurement obtain a signed agreement covering confidentiality, permitted use, safeguards, incident reporting, subcontractor obligations, and data return or deletion. Add a data processing addendum when Legal determines it is needed. For critical hosting, include recovery objectives, restoration evidence, and the right to obtain assurance evidence.
5. Procurement releases access only after the business owner and Security & Risk record approval, Legal confirms required terms are signed, and conditions have owners and due dates. Use synthetic test data while approval is pending.

## While the vendor has access

- The business owner reviews critical vendors quarterly and high or moderate vendors at least annually. Security & Risk reassesses vendors before renewal and whenever the data, service, access, or subcontractors change materially.
- A vendor must report a suspected incident affecting Alderline or municipal data within 24 hours of discovery under the proposed contract standard. Security coordinates evidence preservation, incident triage, and any customer notifications with Legal and the responsible municipality.
- The business owner tracks corrective actions. An independent reviewer checks the evidence before closing each finding.

## When a vendor leaves

Revoke vendor identities and tokens by the end of authorized access. Obtain a documented export or transfer if needed and written confirmation of deletion or a documented retention exception. Reconcile remaining accounts and stored data before closing the vendor record.

## Exceptions and approval

Security & Risk records the scope, rationale, compensating controls, owner, and expiration of proposed exceptions. The COO approves exceptions after Legal and Security review. Exceptions cannot waive customer, contractual, or legal requirements. Record the approval before granting access.

Track review, approval, publication, and rollout evidence on the **Policy Workflow** tab. The policy owner records the approved version and effective date after all rollout gates pass.
