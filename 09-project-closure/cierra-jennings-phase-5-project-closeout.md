## Proposed ongoing monitoring

| Cadence | Proposed owner | Review and escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Weekly during remediation | GRC and action owners | Review milestone aging and evidence blockers. Escalate missed critical targets to Independent Oversight. |
| Monthly during operation | IT Asset Management, Legal and HR | Review license coverage, inventory reconciliation, offboarding and training against the workbook targets. Record the period, source, and denominator where applicable. |
| Monthly and at expiry | Independent Oversight | Review exceptions in use for authority, rationale and expiration. Route unauthorized or expired deviations for resolution. |
| After material changes and annually | Policy owners and GRC | Review policy applicability and controls after software, access, or reporting-process changes. Record owner decisions and update the risk assessment as evidence warrants. |
| At each control test cycle | GRC and independent reviewer | Record coverage, exceptions and remediation status. Reassess residual likelihood and impact only when the evidence supports a rating. |

## Lessons learned

- **Provenance belongs before deployment.** The scenario illustrates how acquiring software outside an approved process can bypass ownership, licensing and security review. Document each decision before release and reconcile installations afterward.
- **Independent escalation needs a defined route.** When people implicated in a decision receive the only escalation, an independent reviewer may never see it. Define who receives the report and who follows up on non-retaliation.
- **Risk ratings and control results serve different purposes.** Inherent scores guide priorities. Synthetic exceptions help design useful tests. Verified control performance and evidence-backed residual scores require their own records.
- **Closure depends on repeatable evidence.** Assign ownership and acceptance criteria at the start. Preserve enough detail for an independent reviewer to reproduce the result and challenge unresolved exceptions.

## Operational closure decision

The proposed independent approver should record the decision, approval date and supporting evidence after Legal resolves the rights and deployment path, action owners supply acceptance evidence, and the reviewer retests affected controls. Record the disposition of each finding and open action. Route any remaining residual risk to the designated risk authority and document the decision, rationale and review date. Keep items with insufficient evidence open and assign their next action and accountable owner.
