# Incident assessment
Status: completed desk-based scenario analysis. No forensic evidence collected.

## Confirmed scenario facts
Derek contributed to a customer-service system central to a smaller firm's business. No separate ownership agreement was signed there; the new employer required one. Horace warned Derek before installation. Derek installed an adaptation, improved efficiency, and was offered wider deployment. Derek proposed negotiating with the former employer; management rejected that proposal and indicated deployment would proceed regardless of his participation.

## Assumptions and evidence gaps
The assignment stipulates regular employment, development within scope, employer resources and no contrary ownership agreement. Transfer method, access rights, deployment extent, registration, existing controls, actual losses and legal consultation are unknown. Do not infer actual dismissal, enterprise rollout or missing technical controls.

| Hypothesis | Evidence to request |
|---|---|
| Retained source code or inappropriate access | Repository access logs, asset return records, endpoint evidence |
| Weak software installation governance | Installation logs, approval tickets, privilege configuration |
| Inadequate IP awareness | Training and policy acknowledgments |
| Management override | Communications, approvals, escalation records |
| Licensing review bypass | Procurement records, license inventory |

## Root cause judgment
The observed initiating event is unauthorized installation following inadequate consideration of stakeholders and authorization. Management's refusal to negotiate is a documented escalation factor. Technical and process root causes remain hypotheses pending evidence. The smaller firm faces the greatest potential harm because the system is central to its business; financial and legal capacity are not established.

## Immediate response
Suspend further deployment and coordinate safe suspension of existing use with legal and IT. Preserve records, identify copies and dependencies, review ownership and licenses, and escalate independently of implicated management. Avoid destructive evidence removal.
