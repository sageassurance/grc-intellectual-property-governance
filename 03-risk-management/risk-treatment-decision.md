# Risk treatment decision
Status: completed qualitative scenario recommendation; residual risk unverified.

## Alternatives
A: disclose origin, suspend unauthorized use and expansion, refuse further participation, notify the former employer promptly if internal escalation fails, and pursue a license or independently developed replacement.
B: continue under management direction, retaining immediate productivity and career benefits while increasing potential infringement, reputational and financial exposure.

## Decision
Recommend A. Duty ethics supports respecting ownership and professional integrity regardless of business convenience. A least-overall-harm analysis also favors intervention: temporary costs and delays are preferable to potentially severe harm to the smaller firm and escalating exposure for the larger firm. A negotiated license could benefit both.

## Strongest objection
Refusal may jeopardize Derek's employment without stopping deployment because management can assign another employee. This is a substantive livelihood and effectiveness concern. Independent escalation, documented objections, non-retaliation safeguards and timely notice to the rightful owner address the limits of individual refusal. Derek should seek legal guidance on safe reporting; Horace should give Derek a prompt opportunity to correct the situation, then report factual concerns to an appropriate representative if Derek does not act.
