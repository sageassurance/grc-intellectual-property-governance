## Accountability
Derek's initial decision represents professional negligence in this assessment: efficiency was his stated motivation, but he disregarded stakeholder interests and a warning. Management bears greater responsibility for knowingly pressing ahead after ownership concerns were raised. The smaller firm has preventive responsibility, not responsibility for another party's unauthorized use. Missing agreements do not negate employee work-for-hire ownership. Resource asymmetry may make enforcement harder; actual resources and motives are unknown.
